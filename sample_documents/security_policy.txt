TECHCORP SECURITY POLICY DOCUMENT
==================================

Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
Last Updated: February 2026
Version: 4.1

1. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Purpose: Establishes information security standards and controls for all TechCorp employees and contractors.

Scope: Applies to all company data, systems, and networks regardless of location or format.

Information Classification:

Level 1 - Public:
- Marketing materials
- Published blog posts
- Public documentation

Level 2 - Internal:
- Internal memos
- Process documentation
- Project plans

Level 3 - Confidential:
- Financial records
- Customer data
- Trade secrets

Level 4 - Restricted:
- Passwords and encryption keys
- Personal identification information
- Executive communications

---

2. ACCESS CONTROL

User Authentication:
- Strong passwords: minimum 12 characters, uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) mandatory for all users
- Account lockout after 5 failed attempts

Authorization:
- Role-based access control (RBAC) implemented
- Principle of least privilege enforced
- Access reviews conducted quarterly

---

3. DATA PROTECTION

Encryption:
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Key rotation every 90 days

Data Retention:
- Operational data: 7 years
- Backup data: 1 year
- Audit logs: 3 years

---

5. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

Regulatory Compliance:
- HIPAA: For healthcare-related data
- GDPR: For European user data

- ISO 27001: Information security management
