3. BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION

Paid Time Off (PTO):
- Years 1-2: 15 days
- Years 3-5: 20 days
- Years 5+: 25 days

Health Benefits:
- Medical insurance (80% company paid)
- Dental insurance (100% company paid)
- Vision insurance (100% company paid)
- Mental health coverage included

Retirement:
- 401(k) matching up to 6% of salary
- Company match vests over 4 years

Stock Options:
Eligible employees receive stock options based on role and tenure.

---

4. LEAVE AND TIME OFF

Sick Leave:
- 10 days annually
- Medical certification required for absences over 3 days

Parental Leave:
- Birth parent: 12 weeks paid
- Non-birth parent: 8 weeks paid

Holidays:
21 company holidays including New Year's, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas, and others.

5. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Training and Education:
- Annual learning budget: $2,500 per employee
- Online courses, certifications, conferences covered
- Internal training programs available
- Mentorship program

Career Development:
- Career development plans created with managers
- Internal mobility encouraged
- Promotion considered annually
